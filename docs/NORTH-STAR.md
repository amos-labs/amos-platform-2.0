# AMOS Labs — North Star

**Rev A · 2026-07-01 · Internal compass. Not a roadmap. Read before making any decision that trades short-term revenue against the far goal.**

## The far destination

An economy where work is priced by **verified outcomes**, not labor time. When AI does the work, verification becomes the scarce good. The atomic unit of that economy is the **proof receipt**: intent, policy, validation plan, evidence, verdict — one portable, auditable object. Whoever operates the gate that verified work flows through operates the rails of that economy. That is the position AMOS is building toward.

## The current vehicle

**The company brain.** Businesses live in AMOS — apps, data, engines — operated by whatever AI they already use, with proofgate on every move. This is not a detour from the destination; it is the demand engine the April protocol lacked. Every tenant, every receipt, every reseller compounds toward the far goal.

## What carries forward from the protocol era

The receipt schema. The Oracle/semantic gate. The trust ladder (reputation earned from passed receipts). Nothing else. Decay curves, emissions schedules, pool separation — defensive mechanism design for a market that didn't exist. Dead until proven necessary.

## Triggers that reopen the marketplace

Do **not** build marketplace/protocol features until one of these is observed, unprompted, from real customers:

1. A tenant asks to hire capability their own AI can't provide (natural form: a bounty with a validation plan, settled in dollars).
2. Receipts get handed across org boundaries as the deliverable itself (Nuvola is closest today).
3. Two AMOS tenants want to transact with each other and neither trusts the other's records.

Trigger 3 — and only trigger 3, at scale — reopens the token question.

## The decision test

At any fork: *does this choice make receipts more numerous, more trusted, or more portable?* If yes, it's on the road. If it just makes the CRM a nicer SaaS, it's drift — acceptable only if it pays for the road.

## What we steer by this quarter

Construction CRM shipped on the platform → agent execution env + receipt generation closed (dogfood P1/P2) → component library extracted → reseller channel live. Everything else trails.
