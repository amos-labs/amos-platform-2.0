**One-paragraph summary:** the mission is **capture-resistant rails** for the agent economy — work priced by *verified outcomes*, with the **proof receipt** (intent, policy, validation plan, evidence, verdict) as the atomic unit. The current vehicle is **the company brain** (businesses live in AMOS, operated by their own AI, Plumbline on every move) — the demand engine that earns neutrality through volume. The stack is decomposed by **lock-in clock**: early-locking layers (open receipt schema — see [`docs/protocol/receipt-schema.md`](protocol/receipt-schema.md) in this repo — open gate, portable reputation) stay open *now* as standing policy; the late-locking layer (settlement / token / marketplace) waits until volume makes neutrality real rather than declared, and is developed as an active side track at [amos-labs/amos-protocol](https://github.com/amos-labs/amos-protocol).
